A few useful words

Digraph
A pair of letters processed together.
Key square
The 5 × 5 grid that decides where every letter moves.
Wrap-around
When a letter at an edge continues from the opposite edge.
Filler
An X (or Q for XX) added to separate repeated letters or complete a pair.
